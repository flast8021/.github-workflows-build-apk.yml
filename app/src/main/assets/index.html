Trade Calculator

Fees are % (0.02 = 0.02%). A negative fee means a rebate. TP is a limit order (maker fee) and SL is a market order (taker fee). Funding rate is per funding interval (usually 8h). Funding is only paid if your position is open when it settles, so set Funding payments to how many times you expect to hold through it (0 = not included in P&L). Cost (MEXC Margin) is the "Margin" MEXC shows next to Open Long. It includes the reserved open and close fees. Gross P&L and ROE should match the exchange. Net P&L is what you actually keep after fees. The liquidation price is an estimate. Your exchange's figure is the one that counts.
