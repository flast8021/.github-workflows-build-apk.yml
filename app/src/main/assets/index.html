Setups

How coins are chosen: volume and spread filter, then "in play" (volume at least 1.2x normal and volatility not shrinking), then price must be near a level (1H/4H swings, previous day and week highs and lows, daily and weekly opens, equal highs and lows).
Steps to Ready: Approaching → At level → Swept (wick past the level, back within 1–2 candles, not a breakout) → Broke structure (a strong or acceptable close through the last swing) → Retesting (pullback into the break point, the last opposite candle or the level) → Ready only when a 5m/15m candle closes back from that zone with a rejection. Entry is that close.
Playbooks: Dual (15m and 5m both broke structure), Standard 15m, and Fast 5m (needs a solid level, a clean sweep and a strong break; half risk). Reversals need a strong level, a clean sweep, a strong 15m break and R:R 2+, at half risk. Range setups must be at the 1H range edge and target the range midpoint first.
Stop: beyond the lowest of the sweep extreme, retest low, level zone (and range edge) plus 0.5 x 15m ATR, never closer than 1 x 15m ATR. Size shrinks so the account risk stays the same. Four other stop distances are tracked in Scanner performance for comparison.
Targets: T1 nearest obstacle, T2 first meaningful level (the target used), T3 major liquidity beyond. A setup is rejected if a strong level blocks the path, the target needs more than 1.2x the coin's average daily range, or the R:R after fees is below your minimum. 2R is only used when nothing is in the way.
Ratings: Market, Level, Trigger and Trade plan are each scored out of 100. Ready needs all four at 50+, and the grade comes from the weakest one (A 70+, B 55+, C below), so one weak part cannot hide behind strong ones. Market regime (BTC trend, volatility, alt strength, funding) is recorded with every signal.
Retest volume: a pullback on lighter volume and a confirming candle on heavier volume adds to the Trigger rating. History on each card shows how often similar past signals reached T1, T2 and T3 (information only). Ready vs Watching (v4.4): a setup is only dropped when it is dead (invalid, expired, missed, not a real sweep, no room). If it is alive but something stops it being tradeable (R:R, an obstacle, a playbook rule, crowded funding, thin order book) it stays in Watching with "Needs: ...". Ratings, daily range and volume are warnings only. A swept level keeps being checked for up to 6 hours even after price moves away from it. Always confirm on your own chart.

Scanner performance

Every Ready signal is saved permanently in a database on this phone, with its full plan (entry, stop, target, level, ATR, trend, BTC, session, grade) frozen at the moment it fired. Nothing is deleted automatically. After each scan, waiting and open signals are replayed on the closed candles since the last check (5m where available, then 15m, then 1H): waiting until filled, missed or expired, then open until the stop or target. If the stop and target fall in the same candle the result is Ambiguous and shown as a range, not counted as a win or a loss. Signals the exchange no longer has candles for are Unresolved. After a stop, the price is followed for 24 hours more to see whether the target was reached anyway. A backup is saved inside the app once a day; use Back up to keep a copy elsewhere.

3. Trade Calculator (optional)

Fees are % (0.02 = 0.02%). A negative fee means a rebate. TP is a limit order (maker fee) and SL is a market order (taker fee). Funding rate is per funding interval (usually 8h). It is filled in from the Scanner. Funding is only paid if your position is open when it settles, so set Funding payments to how many times you expect to hold through it (0 = not included in P&L). Cost (MEXC Margin) is the "Margin" MEXC shows next to Open Long. It includes the reserved open and close fees. Gross P&L and ROE should match the exchange. Net P&L is what you actually keep after fees. The liquidation price is an estimate. Your exchange's figure is the one that counts.
